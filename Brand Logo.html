Concept: dome + crescent + star = Islamic identity; the open green book at the base = knowledge, growth & nurture (tarbiyah); minarets frame the dome. Colours are drawn from the site's own navy + gold + green palette. The emblem is a single scalable SVG that stays legible from billboard to 20px favicon.
