## 1) `heroes.png` — صفحة الأبطال (أوّلًا)

**ارفع:** الصورة الفوتوغرافيّة للأبطال الأربعة، ثمّ `style-reference.jpg`

```
Redraw the FIRST picture (the four children standing in a row) as a hand-painted 2D children's storybook illustration, using ONLY the drawing style of the SECOND picture: the same soft painterly brushwork, warm colours, gentle cartoon faces and outlines.
Do NOT copy anything else from the second picture: no path, no mosque, no palm trees, no prayer rug, no walking pose.
Keep from the first picture: the same four children, the same left-to-right order, standing still side by side facing the viewer, the same clothes and the same height order.
Replace the background with a plain light cream background, nothing else.
Make the heights very clear:
YUSUF (left), boy aged 9, the tallest.
MARYAM, girl aged 7, clearly shorter than Yusuf (her head reaches his chin), full light-blue floral hijab covering all hair and neck, long loose light-blue dress.
ANAS, boy aged 5, clearly shorter than Maryam (his head reaches her shoulder), short white thobe, small white kufi.
MUHAMMAD (right), a three-year-old toddler — not a baby — standing on his own, the smallest, his head reaching Anas's chest, chubby cheeks, tiny white thobe, tiny white kufi, holding Anas's hand.
All four wear simple sandals. This must be a drawing, NOT a photograph, NOT photorealistic, NOT 3D. Absolutely no text, letters, names or numbers anywhere.
```
